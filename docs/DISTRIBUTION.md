# Distribution policy

ArcShot is **open source**: you may read, build, modify, and redistribute the code under [LICENSE](../LICENSE).

## What is allowed

- Clone, build, and run locally for personal or internal use
- Fork, modify, and share source or binaries **outside** Apple's storefronts
- Contribute fixes and features back via pull request

## What is not allowed (for everyone except the copyright holder)

- Publishing this codebase, or a derivative app built from it, on the **Mac App Store** or any Apple-controlled marketplace — **free or paid**
- Shipping a rebranded build through TestFlight or App Store Connect as a public product

The copyright holder may distribute their own signed builds separately; that does not grant App Store rights to third parties.

## Why

The public repository is for transparency, learning, and community contribution — not for repackaging on Apple's store under another name.

If you plan to distribute a fork, read [LICENSE](../LICENSE) and keep the copyright and restriction notices intact.
