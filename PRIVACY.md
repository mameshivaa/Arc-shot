# ArcShot Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-06-26

ArcShot is a local-first macOS application. Your recordings, projects, and exported files stay on your Mac unless you choose to share them elsewhere.

## Data we collect

ArcShot does not operate a cloud backend for recording or editing. We do not receive your screen content, audio, camera video, or project files.

## Permissions

ArcShot may request macOS permissions depending on features you use:

| Permission | Why |
| --- | --- |
| Screen Recording | Capture the display or a window |
| Microphone | Record narration |
| Camera | Record picture-in-picture camera video |
| Speech Recognition | Generate captions locally |

You can deny optional permissions and still use parts of the app.

## Data storage

Projects are stored in the app sandbox under your user account. Temporary capture files may be created during recording and can be cleared from the project library.

## Third parties

ArcShot does not sell personal data. Future versions of this policy will list any third-party SDKs if they are added.
